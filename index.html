Dongjie Yu 余冬杰

I am a PhD student at the School of Computing and Data Science, The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Jia Pan in the PANDA Lab.

My research focuses on robot learning for manipulation, especially imitation learning and reinforcement learning. I am interested in improving learned robot policies through RL post-training.

I interned at Shanghai Qizhi Institute, working on RL post-training for imitation policies, and at Xiaomi Robotics in 2025, working on teleoperation with ViTai tactile sensors.

I received my M.S. (2023) and B.S. (2020) in Mechanical Engineering from Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Shengbo Li. I also collaborated with Prof. Jianyu Chen on safe RL and optimal control.
